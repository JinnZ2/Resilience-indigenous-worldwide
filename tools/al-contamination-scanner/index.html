FIELD ESCALATION PROTOCOL cheapest test first
TIER 1 — spot test $0 · minutes
visual: bright uniform silver = lower risk. grey / mottled / dark oxide, visible inclusions, solder or paint residue = high carryover risk.
magnet: aluminium is non-magnetic. ANY pull = steel/Fe in the melt → embrittlement vector.
acid spot (vinegar, 10 min on inner surface): darkening / pitting = reactive Cu/Pb.
TIER 2 — DC conductivity ~$50
4-point or cheap meter on a wire sample. Decisive for ELECTRICAL only.
IACS < 55% = STOP (below conductor floor). 55–58.5% = derate / escalate.
TIER 3 — handheld XRF ~$1.5–3k
quantitative per-element wt% → full injury readout. MANDATORY before any food or medical use.
HARD RULE. Pb and Cd are invisible to tier-1 tests. A clean-looking batch can carry lethal lead from painted or soldered scrap. Food / medical use → tier-3 XRF, no exceptions.
