# Shareholder Notice — Undisclosed Material Liabilities

Legal template for indigenous coalition use. Targets institutional limited
partners (pension funds, university endowments) whose capital is deployed
into extraction operations affecting indigenous territory without FPIC.

---

## Context: The "LP Squeeze" Strategy

To dismantle a structure as legally fortified as the Extraction Architecture,
indigenous communities require lawfare specialists who can pierce corporate
veils and challenge the Bilateral Investment Treaty (BIT) framework.

### Corporate Accountability Specialists

- **EarthRights International (ERI)** — Foreign Direct Liability; Torture
  Victim Protection Act suits against extractive parent companies.
- **Center for International Environmental Law (CIEL)** — challenges
  "Sovereign Restitution" clauses as violations of jus cogens norms.

### BIT-Breakers (Strategic Litigators)

- **Debevoise & Plimpton** — counter-arbitration against ISDS tribunals;
  Netherlands-Venezuela BIT expertise.
- **International Rights Advocates (IRAdvocates)** — contingency suits
  attacking chain of title where colonial-era concessions lacked FPIC.

### European Enforcement Pivot

- **Prakken d'Oliveira (Netherlands)** — suing Dutch-registered SPVs in
  The Hague; experience with Shell Nigeria precedent.

## Template Shareholder Letter

```
URGENT SHAREHOLDER NOTICE: UNDISCLOSED MATERIAL LIABILITIES

TO: Shareholders and Limited Partners of [Hedge Fund/Entity Name]
CC: Compliance Department; Chief Risk Officer
DATE: [DATE]
SUBJECT: Material Risks Regarding the [DELEGATION NAME]

Dear Shareholder,

We are writing to formally notify you of significant, undisclosed material
risks associated with [Hedge Fund Name]'s participation in [OPERATION].

1. TITLE VOIDANCE AND LEGAL FRAUD
The assets targeted are subject to Adverse Claims of Original Title by the
[PEOPLE'S NAME] Peoples. The "Sovereign Restitution" basis is fraudulent
under international law (UNDRIP) — no Free, Prior, and Informed Consent
(FPIC) was obtained. Your investment is collateralized by contested property.

2. CRIMINAL COMPLICITY (ROME STATUTE / EU CSDDD)
Security contractors with documented human-rights violations are in use.
Any violence, displacement, or contamination in the Security Zone may be
attributed to the fund under the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence
Directive and the U.S. Alien Tort Statute.

3. BREACH OF FIDUCIARY AND ESG MANDATES
Participation violates standard ESG covenants and creates reputational
exposure that could trigger mandatory divestment from institutional partners.

4. FINANCIAL DEBT-MILL INSTABILITY
Reconstruction Bonds are structured as high-risk predatory instruments.
Revenue is so heavily collateralized that long-term sovereign default is
mathematically probable, rendering projected returns unsustainable.

ACTION REQUIRED: Query [Hedge Fund Name] management on their SEC Item 103
(Legal Proceedings) and Item 105 (Risk Factors) disclosures regarding
Clouded Title. Failure to do so may constitute breach of fiduciary duty.

Respectfully,
[Indigenous Coalition / Legal Defense Partners]
```

## Implementation: Timing and Targeting

- Identify anchor investors: large public pension funds (CalPERS, NYSTRS)
  are subject to public pressure and mandatory ESG compliance review.
- Release simultaneously to Financial Times and Wall Street Journal.
- Send at least 6 weeks before the target delegation date so LPs can
  freeze capital drawdowns before the operation commences.
